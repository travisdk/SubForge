SubForge – installation on Linux
================================

1. Copy SubForge.clap to your CLAP plug-in folder:

       mkdir -p ~/.clap
       cp SubForge.clap ~/.clap/

   (or /usr/lib/clap/ to install it for all users).

2. Restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins. SubForge appears as an instrument.

Your presets and patterns are saved in ~/Documents/SubForge/ and settings in
~/.config/SubForge/. Removing the plug-in leaves them in place.

Third-party licences are listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and in the
plug-in under the gear menu > About SubForge.
