SubForge – installation on Linux
================================

The archive contains SubForge in two plug-in formats. Install the one your
DAW uses, or both.

CLAP (Bitwig Studio, REAPER and others):

    mkdir -p ~/.clap
    cp SubForge.clap ~/.clap/

VST3:

    mkdir -p ~/.vst3
    cp -r SubForge.vst3 ~/.vst3/

(Use /usr/lib/clap/ and /usr/lib/vst3/ instead to install for all users.)

Restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins. SubForge appears as an instrument.

Your sound presets are saved in ~/Documents/SubForge/Presets/, patterns in
~/Documents/SubForge/Patterns/ and settings in ~/.config/SubForge/.
Removing the plug-in leaves them in place.

Third-party licences are listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and in the
plug-in under the gear menu > About SubForge.
