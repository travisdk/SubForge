SubForge – installation on Windows
==================================

The archive contains SubForge in two plug-in formats. Install the one your
DAW uses, or both.

For your user only (no administrator rights needed):

    CLAP:  copy SubForge.clap to   %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\CLAP\
    VST3:  copy the SubForge.vst3 folder to   %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\VST3\

For all users (needs administrator rights):

    CLAP:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\
    VST3:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

Create the folder if it does not exist, then restart your DAW or rescan
plug-ins. SubForge appears as an instrument.

Your sound presets are saved in Documents\SubForge\Presets\, patterns in
Documents\SubForge\Patterns\ and settings in %APPDATA%\SubForge\.
Removing the plug-in leaves them in place.

Third-party licences are listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and in the
plug-in under the gear menu > About SubForge.
